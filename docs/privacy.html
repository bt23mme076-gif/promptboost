Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

To improve a prompt, PromptBoost sends the text you choose to improve to its own server, which forwards it to an AI provider and streams back the result. We run no analytics, set no tracking cookies, and do not store your prompts in any database.

1. What We Collect

PromptBoost has no accounts, no analytics, no telemetry, and no crash reporting. We do not build user profiles or sell data to anyone. The only data we process is the prompt text you explicitly choose to improve, and we process it transiently to generate the improved version (see Section 3).

2. Data Stored Locally

The following is stored only in your browser (chrome.storage) and is never uploaded to us:

  • Settings — your chosen AI model and shortcut preferences.
  • Prompt history — a local log of prompts you have improved, kept only on your device. You can clear or export it any time from the extension settings.
  • Pro licence key (optional) — if you purchase Pro, your licence key is stored locally and sent with requests only to verify your plan. Free users have no key.

PromptBoost does not require or collect any third-party API key. The free tier works out of the box.

3. Data Sent to Our Server

When you click the improve button (or use the right-click / keyboard shortcut), PromptBoost sends the following to our server at apipromptboost.atyant.in over an encrypted HTTPS connection:

  • The prompt text you are improving
  • The name of the AI site you are on (e.g. "claude") and a short snippet of the visible conversation, used only to give the rewrite better context
  • Your Pro licence key, if you have one, to authorise premium models

Our server forwards this to an AI provider to generate the improvement: Groq for the free tier, and OpenAI or Anthropic for Pro models. The result is streamed straight back to your browser. Your prompt content is processed in memory and is not written to any PromptBoost database or log. The AI providers process the request under their own privacy policies.

4. Permissions Explained

  • storage — saves your settings, local prompt history, and optional Pro licence key on your device only.
  • activeTab — reads the URL of your current tab (only when you open the extension) to show which AI site you are on.
  • contextMenus — adds a right-click "Improve with PromptBoost" option when you select text.
  • host access (apipromptboost.atyant.in) — lets the extension send prompts to our server to generate improvements.

5. No Tracking or Analytics

PromptBoost contains no tracking pixels, no analytics SDK (no Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Amplitude, or similar), and no error reporting service. We have no visibility into how you use the extension beyond serving the improvement requests you make.

6. Children's Privacy

PromptBoost is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes materially, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be updated. Continued use of the extension after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

8. Contact

Questions about this privacy policy? Open an issue on the GitHub repository or email the developer directly.
